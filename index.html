Marketing attribution for a professional services platform

Hibarr, 2026

The client's data lived in six places, and anonymous website visits never connected to the customers they became. I built an identity bridge in PostgreSQL that resolved anonymous sessions to backend user records, then compared position-based and time-decay attribution models.

Delivered as a nine-page Power BI dashboard with a full data dictionary.

Gold in North America

2026 World Cup prediction system, independent

A full pipeline from raw match data to live probabilities: a Poisson model blending historical form with squad quality, run through 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations of the tournament.

The app tracked its own accuracy after every matchday, scoring itself on Brier score and correct outcomes, and ran through to the final.

About

From buildings to systems

Architecture taught me to read space, structure and constraint, and the relationship between a design and how people actually live inside it. I was always more interested in what drove the decisions than in the decisions themselves.

That curiosity took me to data, and data took me to energy. At Nextier Power I work across the electricity value chain, from generation and grid performance to distribution reform, tariff policy and transition planning, and I try to turn regulatory data into findings a decision-maker can act on.

Outside work, I build things for the pleasure of building them. Nigeria's infrastructure problems are ones I care about personally, not just professionally, and I think the country's energy transition is ultimately a story about people.
